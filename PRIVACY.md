# Privacy

The Vibe Coding SEO & GEO Playbook plugin does not collect, store or send personal data.

- It has no server, no analytics and no tracking.
- The skills only read the website files or pages you point them at, inside your own Claude session, to check SEO and GEO basics.
- Nothing is sent to the author or any third party. Anything Claude reads is handled under your own Claude account and Anthropic's privacy policy.

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/gitnat/vibe-coding-seo-geo-playbook/issues
